The following summarizes modifications from the previous version of this document.

**5**

- Polish the wording of the document throughout.
- Add an introduction to the history of the encoding, and a phonemic analysis of the written languages.
- Remove the transliterations of single-letter written units and phonetic letters. Add _bowed written unit_ in all the letter class tables. Rename the “Lookups” to “Mapping type” in all the Mongolian-specific phase tables.
- Add an account of the Unicode Standard documents that cite this UTN, and of what their citations ask of this document.
- Expand the former toolchain section into a full chapter, describing in detail how to use and maintain this repository.
- Add a chapter on other letters, digits, and punctuation, which were previously not specified.
- For Hudum,
  - Remove the medial variant of <MNG links="Zr"/>, the word <MNG links="m o zr i k2 a n"/> should be <MNG links="m o hh i k2 a n"/>.
  - Add a note for <MNG links="W"/> in letter <MNG links="ee"/> and <MNG links="w"/>.
  - Update the shaping logic for <MNG links="n"/>, <MNG links="t"/> and <MNG links="d"/>.
  - Remove the note for the shaping rules required by EAC. All the shaping rules are now required by GB 25914—2023.
- For Todo,
  - Remove the final variant of <TOD links="Ip"/> and the FVS1 final variant of <TOD links="i"/>, as it is the contextual variant in the ligature. Move the final variant of <TOD links="I"/> to the final variant of <TOD links="I3"/>. Move the final ligated variant of <TOD links="Ip"/> to the final ligated variant of <TOD links="I3"/>.
  - Remove the final variant of <TOD links="N"/>, as it is unattested in Todo. Remove the final variant of <TOD links="Gp"/> (of <TOD links="gh"/>), as it is unattested in Todo. Remove the FVS1 medial variant and the FVS1 final variant of <TOD links="oe"/>, as they are unattested in Todo. Remove the final <TOD links="I3"/> of <TOD links="y"/> and the written unit <MNG links="Y" />, as they are unattested in Todo. Remove the medial <TOD links="O" /> and the final <TOD links="U" /> of <TOD links="w"/>, as they are unattested in Todo.
  - Add the written unit <MNG links="Zz2"/> (of <MNG links="zr"/>), as it is required by the Chinese national standard.
  - Assign FVS2 to the medial <TOD links="N2"/> of <TOD links="n"/>, and add the FVS2 isolated variant of <TOD links="n"/>. Also update the note for <TOD links="N2"/>.
  - Remove the note for <TOD links="ny"/>, as it is found in Todo literature, although it is actually found in a Sanskrit word. Remove the corresponding reference.
  - Treat the letter <TOD links="lvs"/> as a vowel instead of a consonant.
  - Add the missing “2. Syllabic”.
  - Rename the `devsger` in “4. Devsger” to `vowel_devsger`.

**4**

- Fix a typo in the title.
- Update the copyright text in the footer.

**3**

- Add a link to the latest version of the UTN.
- Remove the use of the “word root separator” in Section 2.2.2, and ZWJ is no longer recommended as an indicator for word root boundaries.
- Remove the use of the “word root separator” for Hudum in Table 6, and remove the lookup “toothed” for Hudum in Table 7.
- Remove the use of the “word root separator” for Todo in Table 11, and remove the lookup “toothed” for Todo in Table 12.
- Add GB 25914—2023 as a normative reference for Hudum Ali Gali in Section 7.3.
- Add GB 36645—2018 as a normative reference for Manchu Ali Gali in Section 8.3, and remove WG2 N3041 and WG2 N3092 as references.
- Add GB 36649—2018 as a normative reference for Todo Ali Gali in Section 9.3.
- Fix several typos.

**2**

- Update the title of the document.
- Update the term “writing unit” to “written unit”.
- Complete the second paragraph of Section 2.4, where the original content was missing.
- Update the FVS designation for Todo letter <TOD links="oe"/>. In the medial context, <TOD links="A Ot"/> originally corresponded to FVS1, and <TOD links="O"/> corresponded to FVS2; now <TOD links="A Ot"/> corresponds to FVS2, and <TOD links="O"/> corresponds to FVS1.
